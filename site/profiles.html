Every project in one table: official status, architecture type, the two outcomes and each design cell. Click a column to sort; click a project to open its profile on the map with the decisive documents and quotes. This table is the data behind the map, nothing more — no score, no ranking.

Legend: Y₁ = 1 when the central bank cannot routinely link an online payment to a person (F1 = no); Y₃ = 1 when a route without verified identity exists (F7 = yes). F-columns show the current value; evidence state, commitment status and sources are in the profile. Projects marked real are coded from documents with verified quotes; all others are mock values.
