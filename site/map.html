43 national retail CBDC projects and the digital euro. Colour a project by its official status or by how many official documents we read as evidence, and drag the year to watch the record grow. Click a project for its documents and its privacy index.

Small territories (for example the Bahamas, Aruba, Hong Kong, Macao, Singapore, Mauritius, Palau) are drawn as circles. The euro area is one project: its members share one colour. Countries in a darker grey hold documents we collected but are outside the frame (no national retail project, wholesale only, or a currency union not in the frame). Status is the official one as documented on the date shown in each profile; it is not a result of this dataset.
