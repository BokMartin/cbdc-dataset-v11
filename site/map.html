Architecture types and design features, by year

Every retail CBDC project with an official document, placed in one of six architecture categories (A0–C1) and coloured by any design cell or by its primary declared objective. Drag the year: the map and the chart below show the design as documented at that time, so you can watch categories shift as projects move from options to commitments. Click a country for the full profile — every value with its decisive document, page and quote.

Projects by category over time

Each bar counts projects by the category they were in according to their documents up to that year. A project enters the chart with its first document and keeps its last documented state until a later document changes it; commitment status (option / tested / adopted) is carried in the profile.

Now:

Unit of analysis: one project per decision authority — the euro area, the ECCU and WAEMU are single projects drawn across their member states. Countries in light grey have no official retail-CBDC document in the registry; in darker grey the project is outside the retail matrix (wholesale-only, statutory restriction). Colour conventions in the feature modes: green = the more privacy-protective pole, red = the less protective; F3 and F12 are nominal.
