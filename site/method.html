Method and progress

Written for the supervisor and for ourselves: what the paper now claims, what is finished, what is being decided, and the fixed order of the remaining steps. Status as of 3 November 2026 (v12; the v11 line of 18 October is tagged v11-dev-final in the repository).

What the paper is now

A measurement and data paper (target: Data & Policy): an audited, cross-project account of who is documented as able to link a retail CBDC payment to a person, under what decision and implementation status, with what uncertainty, from official documents only. Three layers, never mixed: the commitment matrix (cells admissible only when documented and endorsed by the competent body or operational), the privacy-posture layer (a P0–P3 ladder and nine indicators over every located statement, status attached), and linguistic emphasis (descriptive only). The table of commitments that disappear under strict rules is the bridge between them.

Why v12: the external consultation (28 Oct) showed that v11 treated conditional statements as commitments and applied a genre-based source rubric unevenly; an independent audit (2 Nov) found the executable instrument did not enforce the proposed rules; a uniform gap check (2 Nov) found 354 official documents the rubric had excluded across the 43 projects, most of them status decisions, legislation and FAQs with explicit privacy statements. A classified institutional illustration is not feasible on the commitment matrix on current evidence (sealed census: 5 model-labelled endorsed positions of 43); the authors decide on 6 Nov whether it is omitted or exploratory.

Step by step (v12)

  1. Project framedone · 2 Nov
    48 projects → 46 national → 43 retail candidates; unions out of the national analysis; exclusions with reasons.
  2. Source rule and gap checkrule drafted · check run 2 Nov
    Authority/content rule replaces genre exclusions. All 43 projects searched: 378 candidates, 354 not in the registry, major gaps in 27 projects. Next: author decision over 178 priority rows, download, SHA-256, dated additions, page texts; then one source manifest (URL, hash, language, route, reuse terms) frozen with a single as-of date.
  3. Language ledgerkept · 2 Nov
    106 English / 19 non-English of the 125 v11 core documents (nine registry labels were wrong). Routes A/B/C/D; issuer English editions ingested for Norway; evidence available only via translation in 7 documents of 6 projects within the frame.
  4. Measurement contract v12draft
    Endorsement by the competent body; decision status × implementation status; symmetric admissibility implemented and tested; no inference from silence; locators with spans; posture layer. Adopted only after the corpus is completed and the sealed census re-run.
  5. Development cases NO and UKdone · 2 Nov
    Complete-coverage single vs two-stage runs; Norway re-run from the issuer's English editions. Values agree on raw cells; under the admissibility gate Norway is undetermined in all four runs and the UK is determined only in single mode. Single mode mislocated five pages in a 1.2-million-character request; two-stage located 17/17.
  6. Sealed model-only screensdone · to be re-run
    Endorsement census and statement survey (2,628 typed statements, 80 % located verbatim), per-project results sealed off-repository, aggregates committed, release governed by an exposure allowlist enforced in code.
  7. Freeze and registrationgate
    Manifest, contract, prompts, routing and gates frozen; Zenodo DOI; OSF registration with exposure disclosure and both language denominators; smallest effect of interest elicited only if a classified estimate remains.
  8. Human referenceafter the gate
    Blind reference workbooks without model columns for a probability sample of 12–16 projects, locked before any model output is opened, plus source-first F1 for every analytic project.
  9. Production and auditafter the reference
    Frozen pipeline, two providers, one scored run on the blind set, challenge battery, outer audit, stability envelope on six fixed projects.
  10. Analysis and releaselast
    Commitment matrix, posture layer, emphasis layer, change map, missingness bounds; every number replayable from archived outputs; this site's mock data replaced by pipeline output.

Open decisions (6 November)

1

Shape of the paper. Measurement-and-data core; the institutional illustration omitted or exploratory on the status-attached view, labelled as such.

2

Order of work. Complete the corpus under the new source rule before any instrument freeze; re-run the sealed census on the completed corpus.

3

Capacity-bound choices. Blind set 12 vs 16; retention of F9/F10; routing rule for very long requests; translations for Chile, Taiwan, Czechia.
