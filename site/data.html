Not redistributed

The documents themselves and the texts derived from them belong to their issuers. We publish their locations and hashes, so anyone can fetch the same file and check it is the one we read. The Internet Archive link on each row helps when an issuer moves a file.

The one exception is our English machine translations of documents whose issuer publishes under an open licence (at present the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan), under the Open Government Data License, version 1.0). They are shown with the issuer's licence and a warning that they were not checked by a reader of the source language.
