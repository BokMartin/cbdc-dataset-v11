Mock (illustration only)

site/assets/data.json is generated by scripts/site/build_site_data.py. For 54 of 56 projects the cell values, types and trajectories are placeholders drawn with a fixed seed and loosely shaped by project status; they are labelled provenance = "MOCK" in the file and on every panel. They will be replaced by the pipeline output without changing the site.
