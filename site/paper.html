The paper

Bok, M. and Stroukal, D. (2026). Privacy in retail central bank digital currencies: what 44 projects say and what they have committed to. Title of the draft. Manuscript in preparation for Data & Policy.

Not yet published here. The manuscript will be posted on this page, with its PDF and a citation, once the authors release it. Until then, the method, the decisions and the code are open in the repository, and the preliminary results are on the privacy index, cell history, paragraphs and PET pages.

What the paper asks

When the makers of a retail central bank digital currency say it will respect privacy, what exactly do they say, and what have they committed to? The paper measures both layers from the official documents of 44 projects and keeps them apart: a stated-design privacy index with intervals, and the volume and substance of privacy talk beside it.

How to cite the dataset
