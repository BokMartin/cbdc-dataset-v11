What do central banks say about CBDC privacy, and what have they committed to?

An open dataset, in development, built only from official documents of the bodies that design retail central bank digital currencies. Every value will lead to a document, a page and a sentence. Two layers are kept apart: what a project says about users' privacy, and what it has committed to.

Explore the mapBrowse the documents
Work in progress. The corpus is assembled and every evidence document has been decided by an author. The coding of the documents has not been run, so this site shows the corpus and its provenance, not results.
