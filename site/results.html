Privacy index

What the official documents of each project state about its design, read as a privacy index from 0 (the design lets the central bank, intermediaries and the state attribute payments to a person) to 1 (it does not). It is an interval: a design question the documents leave open counts as 0 at the lower end and 1 at the upper end. How much a project talks about privacy is shown beside the index, never inside it.

The hypothesis: much talk, few commitments

The thresholds were recorded before the production run (draft Registration A §3); how the three models are combined was not, and the authors have not yet decided it. Much talk: own-voice privacy talk in at least two publications and at least 1 relevant sentence per 100. Few commitments: fewer than 2 of F1, F2, F4 and F7 committed. It holds if at least 9 of the 16 core projects meet both at the least favourable reading (L), fails if at most 8 meet both at the most favourable reading (U), and is inconclusive otherwise. No significance test. Commitments are counted from the same cells as the index; no verdict is given until the authors have verified the core projects and chosen the combination rule.

How to read this page

Seven cells. F1 the central bank can link an online payment to a person; F2 an intermediary can; F4 private offline payments are available; F7 a low-identity route (anonymous holding and paying); F8 identity is required at onboarding; F9 targeted re-identification is possible; F10 programmable restrictions. Less access, less identification and no re-identification mean more privacy. The four-cell variant uses F1, F2, F4 and F7. F3, the lawful access route, has no direction and is shown as a category.

Three models. Each cell was read from the documents by three language models independently (Claude, GPT and DeepSeek). Provisional: a cell has a value when at least one model gives one and no model gives another. Strict: only when all three give the same value, with no condition on the evidence or the quotation. Neither is the rule frozen before the run (ADR-012 D2: all three agree, the evidence is documented and the quotation is located), which leaves far fewer cells with a value. The authors will decide the rule and verify the cells against the sources; until then both readings are shown, and the projects under verification are withheld.

Talk. Own-voice sentences about users' privacy per 100 eligible sentences of the project's evidence documents (lower and upper count); the share of them that state a mechanism or a decision; and how many of the six aspects with a design cell the project talks about without a commitment.
