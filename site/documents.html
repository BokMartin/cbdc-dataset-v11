Every official document we collected for the 44 retail CBDC projects, and what we did with it. Each row links to the issuer's own copy and to an archived copy at the Internet Archive. For documents not in English there is a link to a translation service and, where the issuer's licence allows reuse, our own English machine translation. We do not host the documents themselves: they belong to their issuers.

What the decisions mean

The rule is in SOURCE_RULE_D1. Every decision on an evidence document was made or confirmed by an author. Most companion and "not used" rows still rest on a model's proposal and are labelled as such until an author confirms them. Titles marked "from file name" are placeholders until the title is checked against the document.
