# OCR of the Jamaica FAQ booklet

`JM_JM-BOJ-CBDC-FAQs-Booklet-2022` ("JAM-DEX CBDC FAQs Booklet", Bank of Jamaica, Aug 2022; T1, BORDERLINE) is a scan: no page
has a text layer, so the document was listed as not unitised. Jamaica is a core project, so it is read by OCR, locally and at no
cost (tesseract 5.3.4, English model, 300 dpi). The PDF supplied by MB has the SHA-256 of the registry (`60c22f04…2460a35`).

Method: `scripts/frame/ocr_pdf.py --layout blocks`. The booklet consists of two-page spreads with a question column beside an
answer column whose left edge moves from question to question; tesseract's own page segmentation read across both columns and
interleaved questions and answers of two pages (checked against the page images). With `--layout blocks` each spread is cut
into halves, each half into bands at blank rows and each band into a question part and an answer part at its widest blank gap;
each part is read on its own, so a question comes before its answer. Parts of at most 15 words with a mean confidence below 75
(the BOJ emblem and the JAM-DEX logo, the cover of page 1) are dropped and counted in the OCR record. Two rules repair glyphs:
a bullet read as a letter or a sign at the start of a line, and a capital I read as a lone vertical bar.

Record: `data/manifest/OCR_2026-11-23.csv` (6 pages, all OCR, mean word confidence 88.0, 1 006 words, SHA-256 of the text).
Units: `scripts/units/add_ocr_units.py` cuts the text with `unitise.units_of()`, the same function as for every other document,
and adds the document to `data/units/UNITS_MANIFEST_2026-11-23.csv` with text mode `ocr_tesseract`: 73 units, 998 words, of
which 10 are candidates for classification (`data/units/CANDIDATES_2026-11-23.csv`: 2 dictionary hits, 4 neighbours, 4
random control). Unit ids of all other documents are unchanged. Unit texts stay outside the repository.

Checked against the page images: every question and answer of FAQs 1 to 18 is present and in order; the privacy answer (FAQ 8,
"Is my digital wallet information kept private?") is word for word correct. Known OCR errors left in the text, none of them in
a privacy statement: "BOI" for "BOJ" (FAQ 1), "Igeta" for "I get a" (FAQ 3), "How do!" for "How do I" (FAQ 9, twice), "Is" for
"is" (FAQ 1), "—-" for "–" (FAQ 3), "is CBDC secure?" in lower case (FAQ 7). Page 6 (back cover) keeps 16 words of logo text.

## Pages 7, 14 and 84 of the BOJ Annual Report 2023 (CAND-132)

The annual report (T1, KEEP, 186 pages, SHA-256 `96ed3de6…dd4a5a`, checked) has a text layer on all but 35 pages. Each of the
35 was rendered and looked at on MB's computer: 8 are blank, 13 are chapter dividers, 2 are covers, 6 are photographs or names,
3 hold short graphic text (values, staff figures, contents). Three hold text in an image only: page 7 (the Governor's letter of
transmittal), page 14 ("2023 Strategic Highlights", which reports that the JAM-DEX roll-out continued, a third wallet provider
and point-of-sale use; the same facts are in the text of the Strategic Plan chapter) and page 84 (a JAM-DEX poster). None of the
35 pages mentions privacy.

MB approved OCR of the three pages. It was run on MB's computer (tesseract 5.4.0, English model, 300 dpi, psm 3; reading order
checked against the rendered pages, no cropping needed), words not corrected. Mean word confidence 93.7, 95.5 and 89.3. Record:
the CAND-132 row of `data/manifest/OCR_2026-11-23.csv`. `scripts/units/add_ocr_pages.py` adds 42 units (s2121 to s2162),
numbered after the document's last unit, so no unit id changes; the document's text mode becomes `reading_order+ocr`. None of
the new units is a dictionary hit; one is drawn as random control.

Known OCR errors left in the text: page 7 "W.1." for "W.I.", "ry" for the signature, "baj.gov" and "bej.org.jm" for "boj";
page 84 "BO3J" for "BOJ", "JAM-DExX" for "JAM-DEX", "ina" for "in a", "Instant" for "instant", "*" for a bullet, and stray
characters from the photograph ("4", "y |", "¥", "|"). Bullets of pages 14 and 84 were not read.
