# Audit brief: what was done between 3 and 13 November 2026, claim by claim

Written by the pipeline author (an LLM, Claude) for an independent auditor. Every numbered claim names the file from which it can be recounted. Section 4 lists the weaknesses the pipeline author already sees; they are there so that the auditor can test them, not so that they are taken as settled. Paths are relative to `repo/` unless they start with `extra/`.

## 1. Where the project stood on 2 November (previous audit)

Research question: when a central bank says its retail CBDC will respect privacy, has it committed to a design in which it cannot link payments to persons, and how does what it says relate to what it commits to. Unit: one CBDC project. Frame: 43 retail-candidate national projects (`data/frame/projects.csv`). Instrument: measurement contract (cells F1 to F12, admissibility rule C, posture layer), `docs/MEASUREMENT_CONTRACT_v12_DRAFT.md`. The audit of 2 Nov and the authors' dispositions: `docs/audits/INDEPENDENT_AUDIT_OPENAI_2026-11-02.md`, `DECISIONS/ADR-002-audit-2026-11-02.md`. Process plan in 24 steps: `docs/PROCESS_v12.md`.

Sealed model-only screens of 2 Nov found that the strict commitment matrix determines the primary outcome for about 5 to 8 of 43 projects. The authors decided to report that as a finding and to add a measured "privacy talk" side to set against commitments.

## 2. What was done, in order

**S1. Source rule and gap check (2 to 3 Nov).** Genre-based exclusions replaced by one inclusion rule (`docs/SOURCE_RULE_D1.md`). Six LLM research agents searched the official channels of all 43 projects: 378 candidate documents (`data/manifest/GAP_CHECK_2026-11-02.csv`). A model pre-screen proposed accept 153, companion 141, verify first 59, reject 14, wholesale 11 (`data/manifest/GAP_CHECK_PRESCREEN_2026-11-03.csv`).

**S2. Download and master folder (5 to 10 Nov).** Documents were downloaded by script and, where sites blocked scripts, through the authors' browser, never bypassing bot protection. Master folder: 777 files = 394 registry files + 369 candidate files + 14 issuer English editions (`data/manifest/MASTER_MANIFEST_2026-11-10.csv`; 392 registry files hash-identical to the registry, 2 differ). 13 candidates could not be downloaded (`data/manifest/MANUAL_DOWNLOADS_2026-11-10.csv`). A container loss on 9 Nov and the restoration from archives is described in `DECISIONS/ADR-003-container-loss-and-restoration-2026-11-09.md`.

**S3. Decisions and tiers (9 to 11 Nov).** One row per document, 772 rows, with `decision_v12`: KEEP 250, BORDERLINE 35, RECHECK 88, COMPANION 222, DROP 152, WHOLESALE 25 (`data/manifest/TIERS_2026-11-10.csv`). Source of the decision: the authors' verdicts of the earlier corpus ("MB v11"), a model pre-screen, a title-level model triage, or an author decision (`data/manifest/AUTHOR_DECISIONS_2026-11-11.csv`, 12 documents). Evidence document = KEEP or BORDERLINE. Project tier by number of evidence documents: T1 ≥ 8 (11 projects), T2 5 to 7 (12), T3 ≤ 4 (21); 29 codes are outside the frame. 17 projects would move up a tier if their RECHECK documents were kept. The authors decided (9 Nov) to draw year-by-year series only for T1/T2. The authors' review workbook: `extra/TIER_REVIEW_v12_2026-11-13.xlsx` (not yet filled in).

**S4. Language (10 Nov).** Page-level language detection of all 777 files (`data/manifest/MASTER_LANGUAGE_2026-11-10.csv`; 495 English). Routes: A English original, B issuer's own English edition, D machine translation (`docs/LANGUAGE_LEDGER.md`). Five research agents checked every queued non-English document for an issuer English edition; decisive cases were verified in a browser (`data/translation/ROUTE_B_PAIRS.csv`: 72 rows, 19 full editions, 7 partial, 2 unofficial, 44 none found; `data/translation/ENGLISH_EDITIONS_2026-11-10.csv`: 14 fetched).

**S5. Machine translation (10 to 11 Nov).** Procedure: `docs/TRANSLATION_PROCEDURE.md`. Queue of T1/T2 non-English evidence documents: 44 to translate, 14 covered by an English edition, 5 benchmark pairs (`data/translation/TRANSLATION_QUEUE_2026-11-10.csv`). One API call per chunk, no history, system prompt `pipeline/prompts/translation.system.md` v1.1, model `gpt-6-astra` (OpenAI). Hash chain source file → request → response → assembled English text (`data/translation/TRANSLATION_REQUESTS_2026-11-10.csv`, `data/translation/runs/*/CALLS.csv`, `data/translation/TRANSLATION_MANIFEST_*.csv`). Run 1: 43 documents, 174 calls; run 2: 4 documents kept from RECHECK, 14 calls. Two request/response pairs are in `extra/translation_examples/` so that the chain can be recomputed.

**S6. Translation benchmark (11 Nov).** The five benchmark originals (three Norges Bank memos, one Bank of Japan report, one Bank of Russia concept) were machine-translated with the same prompt and compared with the issuers' English editions, deterministically (`scripts/translation/benchmark_translations.py`, `data/translation/benchmark/`, table in `docs/TRANSLATION_PROCEDURE.md`): chrF 74 to 84, key-term rank correlation 0.86 to 0.98, numbers found 90 to 100 %, same commitment category 50 to 94 %, same negation 94 to 100 %; 163 sentence pairs matched of 247 privacy sentences. A human judgement of 86 pairs (all 56 flagged, 30 random unflagged, blind to stratum) was registered before judging (`data/translation/benchmark/JUDGEMENT_KEY_2026-11-11.csv`) and is not done yet. The sentence pairs are in `extra/TRANSLATION_BENCHMARK_2026-11-11.xlsx`.

**S7. Privacy-talk pilot (12 Nov, re-computed 13 Nov).** Deterministic dictionary count per sentence on the 250 evidence documents with English text (`scripts/census/talk_pilot.py`, `data/census/TALK_PILOT_aggregate.json`, reading in `docs/PRIVACY_TALK_INSTRUMENT_PROPOSAL_2026-11-12.md` sections 2 and 9). Main results: privacy sentences are about 3 % of sentences; counts follow the number of documents (0.67), shares do not; document kind explains about 19 % of the variance of document salience; the project share of variance (ICC) is 0.13 to 0.16; split-half reliability of a share-based project score is 0.46.

**S8. Unit-based instrument (12 to 13 Nov).** `DECISIONS/ADR-004-unit-based-privacy-talk-instrument-2026-11-12.md` (agreed in principle by one author; the second has not confirmed). Documents are split into numbered sentence units before any model sees them (`scripts/units/unitise.py`; 250 documents, 185 295 units, `data/units/UNITS_MANIFEST_2026-11-13.csv`; 28 evidence documents have no English text, `data/units/UNITS_NOT_UNITISED_2026-11-13.csv`). Candidates for model classification: dictionary hits D 7 037, their neighbours N 5 792, a 5 % hash-ranked random control R 6 123; fragments X 48 233 are excluded (`scripts/units/select_candidates.py`, `data/units/CANDIDATES_2026-11-13.csv`). Draft prompts: relevance `pipeline/prompts/talk_L1_relevance.system.md`, facets `pipeline/prompts/talk_L2_facets.system.md`; schema `schema/talk_units_v13_DRAFT.json` (facets voice, aspect with 10 values, direction, basis, status, modality). No unit has been classified by a model. Planned reporting: a funnel sentences → mentions → own-position statements → statements with a mechanism → endorsed or operational → admissible F-commitments, instead of a difference of two indices. A diagnostic sample of 300 units with their strata is in `extra/UNITS_DIAGNOSTIC_SAMPLE_300.csv`.

**S9. Correction of the unit text (13 Nov).** The first unitisation used the `pdftotext -layout` text, which interleaves the sentences of side-by-side columns. PDFs of routes A and B are now read in reading order; the pilot was re-computed. Machine translations were made from the layout text and were not redone; three are flagged by `layout_gap_share` > 0.15.

**S10. Issuer and voice (13 Nov).** `scripts/review/record_issuers.py`, `data/manifest/ISSUERS_2026-11-13.csv`: 278 evidence documents; 253 central bank, 17 government, 8 legislature; document voice class institutional 197, other classes 81. 121 rows were proposed by the model from title pages (`data/manifest/ISSUERS_PROPOSED_2026-11-13.csv`), 157 are the issuer string recorded at the gap check, normalised by rules. Pre-fill; not confirmed by the authors.

**S11. Human agreement sample (13 Nov).** `scripts/units/draw_agreement_sample.py`, `data/units/AGREEMENT_SAMPLE_2026-11-13.csv`, protocol and thresholds `docs/TALK_AGREEMENT_PROTOCOL_2026-11-13.md`, codebook `docs/TALK_CODEBOOK_cs.tsv` (Czech text, English values), scorer `scripts/units/score_agreement.py`. 30 training units (Norway, United Kingdom) and 400 units for two coders (the two authors), drawn before any model classification: D 260, N 80, R 60; route A 292, B 30, D 78; T1 201, T2 140, T3 59; 42 projects, 151 documents.

## 3. Who decided what

Author decisions: the translation policy (all non-English evidence is translated; no author verifies languages he does not read), the tier thresholds and the restriction of yearly series to T1/T2, 12 RECHECK documents, the benchmark as quality evidence, the unit-based design in principle, keeping a typology of privacy aspects. Model pre-fill awaiting the authors: 135 of the 250 unitised documents are evidence by the model pre-screen only; all issuer and voice classes; the list of ten aspects and its mapping to F-cells; the agreement thresholds.

## 4. Weaknesses the pipeline author already sees

1. **The evidence set is not final.** More than half of the unitised documents are evidence by model pre-screen; 88 RECHECK rows are undecided; tiers of 17 projects can change. The pilot, the candidates and the agreement sample were all computed on this provisional set.
2. **Tier is a function of collected volume.** T1/T2 are the projects that publish most (or were searched best). Any talk measure restricted to T1/T2 is conditioned on publication volume; the gap check's recall is unknown and may differ by language and region.
3. **One translation model, one run, no second-model check.** The benchmark covers three source languages (Norwegian, Japanese, Russian) out of those in the queue; 84 of 247 privacy sentences could not be matched automatically; modal strength was not stable. Translations were made from column-interleaved text where the source had columns.
4. **Unitisation is a regular expression.** It was changed once after results had been computed. HTML sources carry navigation text; table cells become fragments; a "sentence" of a slide deck is not a sentence.
5. **Recall rests on a 5 % random control.** If relevant units are rare outside dictionary hits, the interval on recall will be wide; the dictionary is English and was written by the same model that will classify.
6. **The talk facets and the contract are not aligned.** The contract uses two status axes (decision × implementation, rule B); the talk schema uses a single ladder (CONSIDERED, TESTED, ENDORSED, OPERATIONAL, REJECTED) that the contract retired. The mapping from aspects to F-cells is a sketch. The posture layer of the contract (P0 to P3, G1 to G9) and the unit-based instrument overlap, and no document says which one is in force.
7. **Agreement sample.** The coders are the authors and know the projects. The sample is drawn before the final document set, over-samples dictionary hits and translated text by fixed quotas, and scores facets only on units both coders call relevant. The thresholds were proposed by the model.
8. **Exposure.** Coding 400 units from 42 projects shows the authors source sentences of projects for which they are later supposed to code F-cells blind (`docs/PROCESS_v12.md` step 19, `EXPOSURE_LEDGER.md`). They see no model output, but they are no longer naive about those texts.
9. **Low project signal.** With an ICC near 0.15 a project-level talk score is unreliable for most projects, yet the paper's question compares talk and commitment per project.
10. **Process.** None of the scripts written since 3 Nov has a test (the six test files cover the earlier extraction pipeline). CI failed for a day on a false positive without being noticed. Manifests exist in several dated versions. The registry's `issuer` field held the acquisition batch for 121 evidence documents until 13 Nov.
11. **As-of date.** The contract fixes 18 Oct 2026 as the as-of date; documents were collected and downloaded in November, and some carry later dates.
12. **Literature anchors** of the proposal (section 7) are from the model's memory and have not passed a citation check.
