### 1. BLOCKER · The same cell is an inadmissible claim in one result and a commitment in another

**Items 2, 4, 11, 12, 14, 21.** I passed an invented conflicted, partial, operational record to the supplied functions. `admissible()` returned false, `axis2()` counted one decided cell, and `pair_state()` returned “admissible”. These are executable counterexamples, independent of expert judgement. Evidence: `scripts/derive.py:25–37`, `scripts/derive_index.py:62–101,137–149`, `scripts/talk_commitment.py:38–52`, `scripts/index_reliability.py:27–42,48–76`, `data/index/CODER_TEMPLATE_2026-11-21.csv:1`.

About 1–2 author hours for semantics plus 1–2 engineering days; v12 extraction migration is additional.

### 4. Three-model unanimity and its alternatives

Unanimity measures stability across three configurations sharing text, translation and instructions. One primary plus two sensitivity runs gives a clear production rule, but makes the primary’s systematic errors decisive. Majority reduces adjudication load but two models can share a mistake. Unanimity with an exact span handles some disagreement, but favours easy/common claims and can reproduce shared omissions. Human adjudication of every core cell is the best fit for a 16-project headline, especially because the authors already plan independent passes. Preserve raw first-pass labels and lock the human reconciliation before the three-model comparison.

### 10. Eighty sentences and failure consequences

Eighty **relevant** units can expose major failures for voice (three possible values); ten aspect categories would average eight examples each even under equal frequencies. A category with 1% frequency has about a 45% chance of being absent in 80 independent relevant units. Eighty mixed sentences yield fewer relevant observations. The sample cannot certify rare values. Store the original paired labels, confusion tables, denominators, raw agreement, nominal alpha, AC1, uncertainty and counts by category. Include conditional and end-to-end scores, with U, blanks and abstentions visible.

### 12. Rule B, rule C and implementation

Implementation: distinguish `untested_or_unreported`, `tested`, `operational`, `unclear`. A documented trial can establish `tested` with experiment ID.
