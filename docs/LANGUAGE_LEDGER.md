## Reuse terms (`data/registry/LICENCES_BY_INSTITUTION_2026-11-02.csv`, `DOC_LICENCES_2026-11-02.csv`)

125 core documents: reproduction with attribution 59, all rights reserved / permission required 42, not stated 14, open licence 10 (CC BY 4.0: OeNB, RBA site, Australian Treasury; OGL v3: HM Treasury incl. CP 797/970; OGDL Taiwan; CC0 rijksoverheid; US public domain). Counter-intuitive: Bank of England publications are all-rights-reserved (personal/internal use only; academic permission "typically granted" on request), the RBA pilot reports carry their own all-rights-reserved notice despite the site-wide CC BY, the SARB Khokha reports allow reproduction despite the site's consent rule. Five institutions unverified (UY, MG, MU, BT, AE). Deposit consequence (corrected after audit item 15): the classes record reuse with attribution, not permission to redistribute full files; e.g. the two Helvetia reports are in the attribution class but their notice covers brief excerpts only. Before any Zenodo deposit each document needs separate columns for full-file redistribution / excerpts / adaptation / commercial use; until then no count of openly depositable documents is claimed.
