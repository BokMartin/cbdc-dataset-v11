## Did the chain work

- L1: 300 of 300 responses parsed. Calls, batches, cache, cap, records (`runner.py record`, run record v1 valid) and
  `run_statement.py` work on all three providers.
- Extraction, by the strict check (`v122.check`): 3 of 6 requests pass.
  - **Claude 0 of 2**: both responses are complete and correct JSON, but wrapped in one Markdown code fence (```` ```json ````).
    With the fence removed, both pass the check with 0 rejected observations (14 and 12 kept). The check is not wrong by the
    prompt; whether the parser may remove one outer fence is a decision for MB (no prompt change).
  - **astra 1 of 2**: NO passes (18 observations, 0 rejected). UK: the model marked page 10 (a table) unreadable, left one
    boundary pending and set `complete` to false, so the whole request fails as the check requires.
  - **DeepSeek 2 of 2** pass the envelope, but in NO all 13 observations are rejected because `modality` is `null`, and in UK
    5 of 8 kept (2 quotes not verbatim, 1 segment over 60 words). The prompt's table row puts `modality` under "concise text or
    null" and then says "modality is unconditional, conditional or n/d"; the check accepts only the three values. This
    inconsistency between prompt §4 and the check is for MB to settle (no prompt change made).
- Reducer (`reduce_v122.py`) runs on all three outputs and writes cells; every cell is `unknown` with the search open, as it
  must be with 2 of 25 documents of the two projects.
- `exposure_registry.py` stopped on extraction runs (their unit is a whole document); fixed so that every unit of the document
  counts as model-labelled. 417 blocks now, 266 model-labelled.

## Agreement of the three models (development data, NO and UK; not a result)

- Talk L1, 100 units: all three agree on 83 (Fleiss kappa 0.81). Claude and astra agree on 97, Claude and DeepSeek on 83,
  astra and DeepSeek on 86. Most disagreements (11) are N, N from Claude and astra against R from DeepSeek: DeepSeek marks
  more units relevant (49 R against 36 and 38).
- Extraction, cell and value of the non-context observations (two short documents, so only indicative): NO, all three give
  F11 stated, F12 undecided and the privacy principle stated; Claude and astra also give F4 unspecified and an anonymity
  statement, DeepSeek does not. UK, all three give F4 unspecified, the legal reference and "no decision yet"; astra and
  DeepSeek also give F4 no; Claude alone gives F7 unspecified.
