# Core-document selection rubric (corpus v2.1, 17 Oct 2026)

Goal: keep ONLY documents that are genuinely official design / pilot / policy documents about the issuing institution's OWN CBDC project and that contain concrete design content. Drop general, educational, exploratory-only and communication material.

Verdict (one per document):
- KEEP — official document (central bank, ministry, legislature, government, official project consortium such as BIS Innovation Hub + the central bank) about the institution's OWN CBDC project, containing concrete design/policy content: white/design papers, consultation papers and consultation responses/summaries, technical or working papers describing the own design, pilot/PoC/phase/evaluation reports, legislation/decrees/draft laws, formal board/council decisions, progress/status reports with design content, working-group or stakeholder-group minutes WITH design content, RFP/RFI with requirements, strategy documents with a concrete CBDC design section.
- BORDERLINE — official and about the own project, but mainly exploratory/educational without decisions (e.g., "options for a CBDC" staff study with no stated preference), public-attitude surveys, strategy plans where CBDC is one short section, annual-report chapters with a substantive project description, slide decks with real design content, documents about a cross-border/wholesale experiment the institution co-ran.
- DROP — speeches, interviews, press releases, FAQs/explainers/topic pages, blogs, newsletters, event or competition announcements, "what is a CBDC" educational material, research about OTHER countries' CBDCs or CBDC theory in general, documents where CBDC is a passing mention, anything not issued by an official body.

Also fill: doc_kind ∈ {white_paper, consultation, consultation_response, technical_paper, pilot_report, progress_report, legislation, decision, minutes, rfp_rfi, strategy, survey, annual_report_chapter, speech, press_release, explainer, research_general, other}; own_project (Y/N — is it about the issuer's own CBDC project?); design_content (0 = none, 1 = general mentions, 2 = some concrete design/policy choices, 3 = document is primarily concrete design/pilot content); evidence (one line, cite page numbers or quote fragments from the pack); confidence (high/medium/low).

Rules: judge from the evidence pack first; open the PDF (pdftotext on by_year/<year>/<fname>) whenever the pack is insufficient or the call is close. Wholesale-only documents: judge content normally and add "wholesale" in notes (they stay flagged separately). Documents already out of scope (in_scope != in / wholesale_flag) still get a verdict for completeness. Do not be lenient: Martin's instruction is that the paragraph quality matters more than count.
