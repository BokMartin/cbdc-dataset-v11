# ADR-008: website rebuilt on the real corpus; publication of machine translations under open licences (21 Nov 2026)

Status: decided by MB on 21 Nov 2026 (project thread, decision card "How should the website offer translations of the documents?", option "publish our translations"); DS to confirm. Implemented by the pipeline author on the same day.

**DS confirmed on 22 Nov 2026, relayed by MB in the project chat at 16:08 UTC** ("ano dominik je potvrdil", answering whether DS had also confirmed ADR-008 to ADR-010). The decision is in force for both authors.

## 1. Context

The website in `site/` was a mock-up of October 2026 (contract v1.1, 70 projects, seeded placeholder values for architecture types and design cells). After the frame of 44 projects (ADR-005) and the author decisions on every evidence document, that mock-up showed values that the project has not measured and could be read as results. MB asked on 21 Nov for the best possible open-source site with (a) a map of the projects and (b) a document database that shows which documents were used and which were not, links to every one, and lets a visitor download and translate them.

## 2. Decisions

1. **The site shows the corpus, never results.** It is generated by `scripts/site/build_site_data.py` from the committed manifests (`TIERS_2026-11-17.csv` and the files it names). It shows the 44 projects with their official status and its source, every document with the authors' decision (evidence, companion, not used, wholesale), who made it (an author, or a model proposal not yet confirmed), language, how an English text was obtained, the issuer's URL and the SHA-256 of the file held. It shows no F-cell value, no talk label, no model output of classification, nothing of the sealed screens of 2 Nov, and no per-project dictionary or candidate counts (no ranking by amount of talk). The mock generator, `site/assets/data.json` and the pages that displayed placeholder values (profiles, features, paper, pipeline, contribute) leave through git.
2. **Download = link.** Documents are not hosted. Each row links to the issuer's URL and to the Internet Archive (`web.archive.org/web/<url>`), and gives the SHA-256 so a reader can check the file.
3. **Translation.** For every document not in English the visitor gets links to translate it with a third-party service (Google Translate for HTML pages, file translation in Google Translate or DeepL for PDFs); the site sends those services nothing. **MB decided in addition to publish the project's own English machine translations**, which amends the rule in `AGENTS.md` ("translations never enter the repository") as follows: a translation may be published on the website only when the issuer publishes under an open licence that permits adaptation and redistribution, the file is byte-identical to the latest translation record in `data/translation/TRANSLATION_MANIFEST_*.csv`, and it is listed in `data/site/PUBLISHED_TRANSLATIONS_<date>.csv` with the licence and its source. The build fails on an unlisted file or a hash mismatch.
4. **First list (21 Nov 2026): nine documents of the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan)**, licence Open Government Data License, version 1.0 (`data/registry/LICENCES_BY_INSTITUTION_2026-11-02.csv`, confidence high). Not included: the letter of the Dutch Minister of Finance (rijksoverheid.nl declares CC0, but the document sits on officielebekendmakingen.nl, where no licence was found; confidence medium); all other issuers of machine-translated documents state all rights reserved, reuse with attribution only, or nothing.
5. Every published translation carries a warning that it was not checked by a reader of the source language and that the original is to be quoted, the issuer's licence, and the model and run that produced it.

## 3. Consequences and open points

- MB's judgement of the translation benchmark (17 Nov) found 30 % of matched pairs "meaning differs"; DS found 4 %; the pairs are not adjudicated. The published texts are labelled accordingly. If the adjudication shows systematic errors, the published list is revisited.
- Publishing translated text is not an exposure of an author to model classification output (EXPOSURE_LEDGER); no ledger row is needed.
- The Dutch letter can be added if an author confirms its licence status.
- 111 documents (mostly registry rows of the v11 corpus) had no recorded title. MB asked on 21 Nov to fill them from the texts: titles were read from the first pages of the held files (text cache of 21 Nov) and recorded in `data/manifest/DOC_TITLES_2026-11-21.csv` with `checked_by_author = no`; the site marks them as read by a model until an author checks them.
- MB set the final destination of the site: cbdcdataset.org (21 Nov).
- The repository `bokcvok-droid/CBDCdataset.org-WIP-version` is empty; where the site is published (GitHub Pages of this repository, or that repository as the public copy for cbdcdataset.org) is for MB to decide.
