## 3. Consequences the authors should see before deciding

1. **Time.** The auditor's sequence up to the first production call has about a dozen steps with author input (scope, C0, metadata, U rule, three rounds, confirmation set, 400-unit relevance reference by both authors, 120-unit blank facet coding). A talk layer run on three models by 30 November is possible only if these are done within about ten days; the commitment layer is not.
2. **Money.** Without page packing and with the provider's default reasoning the talk layer on three models is estimated at 350 to 850 USD (Batch API where offered; the range is the unmeasured reasoning usage). The capability probe of the auditor's step 8, a few dollars, replaces this estimate with a measurement before anything else is spent.
3. **Claims.** With voice and aspect independently coded the paper can call relevance, voice and aspect validated and the remaining facets model-coded. Without it every facet is exploratory, and the talk × commitment table and the event history rest on exploratory labels.

## 4. Decisions of MB, 17 Nov 2026 (evening)

1. **No split of the target.** Everything, the commitment layer included, is to be finished by the end of November, mid-December at the latest. (The proposal of row F02 to move F-cells to December as a separate target is not adopted; its technical prerequisites stand.)
2. **Blank coding of voice and aspect (F01): undecided.** MB asks whether it can be done after the extraction, what the minimum is, and whether an exploratory paper is unfit for publication. Answer of the pipeline author, recorded for DS: (a) it can be done after the model runs, on two conditions: the units are reserved now and never shown to the authors with model output, and the authors' labels are locked before they see the model's labels for those units; the risk of running first is financial (a facet that fails validation was paid for), not methodological; (b) the auditor's minimum is 80 to 120 relevant units, two columns, both authors; (c) the auditor does not say that a model-coded descriptive dataset is unpublishable; without the blank coding the paper must call all seven facets exploratory, and its two central outputs (talk × commitment by aspect, event history of statements in the issuer's own voice) rest on exactly the two facets in question.
3. **Page packing is dropped** if that improves quality (F07, F12): one unit per request.
4. **Translation only for documents of T1 and T2 projects.** The 14 untranslated documents belong to T3 projects (CL, CZ, IS, UY, HU, MG, TR) and are not translated. Consequence to be stated in the paper: Chile and Czechia are in the frame with evidence that was not read; no statement about their talk or commitments is made. **Open inconsistency flagged by the pipeline author:** seven documents of today's T3 projects (CN 3, PE 3, TH 1) are machine-translated, because their projects were T2 when the queue was built on 10 Nov and moved to T3 after the authors' rechecks. As it stands, which T3 documents are read in translation depends on the tier a project had on 10 Nov. Options: keep and state it; drop the seven from the measurement; or translate the 14.
5. **The Israel duplicate is linked** (F03): `IL_BoI_2024` is read as `CAND-111`.
6. MB asked for the exact file for row 42, row 71 and the meaning of "U": `CBDC_TALK_v13/TALK_DEV_FIX_MB_2026-11-17.xlsx` in the Dropbox folder (sheets RADKY_42_71 and U_24). The returned workbook of 17 Nov is kept unchanged; labels given after the clarification are stored separately from the blind ones.
